Tangram Puzzles – Privacy Policy

Tangram Puzzles is made by Vikas C. Srivastava.

What we do not collect

The game does not ask for, collect or store your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location, advertising ID or any other personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies.

Anonymous usage counts

To know roughly how many people use the game, it adds 1 to a few shared counters, for example "players today", "players this week", "phones in total" and "puzzles solved". These are plain numbers. No identifier, device details or location is sent, so a count cannot be linked to you or your phone. The counters are kept by the free counting service Abacus (abacus.jasoncameron.dev). Like any website your phone connects to, that service receives your internet address with the request; the game does not send it any other information.

Scores on your phone

Your scores, solved shapes and settings are saved only on your own phone, in the app's local storage. They are never uploaded. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it deletes them.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. For questions, contact the developer through the app's store listing.
